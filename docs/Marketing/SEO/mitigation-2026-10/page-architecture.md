### Section key and action boundaries

Every key named in the matrix is defined here. `lh-intro`, `lc-intro`, and `cl-intro` frame the route page and inquiry eligibility without asserting service operation; retained `iw-intro` and `wi-intro` keep their current introductions subject to editorial coherence review. `lh-arrival-locality`, `lc-arrival-locality`, `iw-arrival-locality`, `cl-meeting-locality`, and `wi-meeting-locality` collect a desired place/address for carrier assessment, not a confirmed stop. `lv-intro` and `if-intro` retain the hub introduction and selection promise. `lh-actual-locality` owns Hannover’s actual-locality FAQ. `lv-select` and `if-select` support existing catalog selection only; `lv-actual-locality` and `if-actual-locality` explain nearby-settlement representation and private disclosure of the real place; `lv-nearby-examples` and `if-nearby-examples` hold the named contextual examples. `lv-outbound-return` and `if-outbound-return` distinguish direction and return preparation. `lh-independent-onward`, `cl-independent-onward`, and `wi-independent-onward` concern separately planned onward transport; `lh-lviv-first-leg` and `lc-lviv-first-leg` concern a separately planned first leg to Lviv. `lc-arrival-orientation` is independent city orientation, not carrier service. `cl-lviv-arrival` and `wi-arrival-coordination` cover desired arrival details and coordination with people meeting the passenger. `iw-shared-departure` is conditional group preparation. `*-questions` and `*-baggage-transfer` preserve questions for case-specific confirmation. `*-changed-plan` / `*-locality-changes` invite renewed agreement if details change. `lv-route-cards` and `if-route-cards` preserve existing catalog cards; `lv-faq-resources` and `if-faq-resources` retain relevant FAQs/resources; `lv-contact-alternative` and `if-contact-alternative` retain the carrier-site path. Every `*-inquiry` key retains the existing page action.

No page implies a reservation, guaranteed connection, direct or plural service, schedule, fare, stop, availability, wait, or confirmed route through a locality. The selected hub is a catalog choice, not a required travel destination. Passenger names, phone numbers, dates, credentials, and message text never enter analytics tracking.
