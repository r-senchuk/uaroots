# S02 frozen passenger evidence packet

**Frozen:** 2026-10-27. Research input for S02 single-Vibe synthesis; not interviews, demand findings, or approved copy. Review date is the packet freeze, not a public content-review date. Sources in product code identify geography/resource links but do not establish transport operations.

## Existing product contract and boundaries

- A traveler can select the nearest available catalog city for an actual smaller-town origin/destination, privately name the real place/address, and ask feasibility. They do **not** have to travel to the selected hub. Carrier confirms feasibility. This is an explicit repository/expanded contract (AGENTS.md; `docs/Operations/Agentic/Operational/repository-contract.md`, nearby-place clarification dated 2026-10-10), not inferred market demand.
- M1 inquiry is discovery/intent. It does not reserve a seat. UARoute does not store names, phones, or travel dates. Do not send these, credentials or message content to `track()` (`AGENTS.md`; `docs/00-project-constitution.md` §§3,8; `docs/Product/passenger-discovery-conversion-spec-2026-10-22.md` §14; `src/lib/whatsapp.ts`). WhatsApp message is composed for user review/send; carrier site is available without date or phone (`src/app/about/page.tsx:26–30`).
- “Commercial” means published inquiry eligibility, not an operated/scheduled service. Route/city selection is fixed and IDs must remain stable (`src/data/routes.ts`; `src/data/city-hubs.ts`; `src/data/discovery.ts`). Nearby names are geographic context, not aliases, stops, routes or coverage promises.
- `src/data/routes.ts` has no standalone Ivano-Frankivsk↔Hannover route page; owner-approved pair remains inline inquiry only. Do not make a new indexable page.

## Claims of evidence, hypotheses, and unknowns

- **Evidence-supported (content/code/contract):** existing city choices, direction-specific pages, route-search behavior, nearby selection explanation, inquiry CTA behavior, date-free carrier-site alternative, and the checklist topics users can ask about.
- **Contract-required:** never imply hub travel is mandatory; carrier confirms trip feasibility/terms/meeting point; no reservation claim; preserve privacy; keep return linking directional; no new route URL from selectable pairs.
- **Hypotheses only:** which nearby locality has demand, whether any landmark is preferred, whether cyclists need further details, whether copy changes improve discovery/conversion, and whether a transfer or return-flow question is common. No interviews or passenger demand data were collected for this packet.
- **Unknown:** actual service path/stop, pickup/dropoff, service date/availability, schedule, fare, baggage limits/cost, bicycle acceptance, transfer practice, vehicle continuity, connection buffer, operator receipt and passenger outcomes.
