## 4. Distribution and measurement package

Prepare three relevant community placements: one Lviv-focused, one IF-focused and one destination/return-focused. Verify channel rules/audience fit; disclose UARoute affiliation. No automated sending or publication is authorized by this document. Link to a matching useful page and preserve approved source/medium/campaign tuples from `src/config/utm.ts`; do not add group/person identifiers to analytics or forward acquisition tags to the carrier.

Maintain a non-passenger placement/release log: date, public destination URL, channel, known exposure, consented external visits and aggregate operator feedback. Do not assume direct traffic is independent passengers or that excluding Tag Assistant removes all testing. No new account settings, analytics events, storage or subscriptions are required.

Weekly measures: per-URL indexing status; settled GSC property/page/query impressions and clicks; production-host GA4 sessions by channel/page; matched-unit sessions with handoffs; separately, privately verified aggregate received inquiries and completed journeys. Never divide click-event counts by sessions and call it a conversion rate, sum overlapping users, or upload passenger data/codes to analytics. Use the [analytics contract](../08-analytics-and-attribution.md).

Review after four weeks from approved publication/distribution start, with longer observation if organic exposure is insufficient. First useful signals are relevant unpaid visits and carrier-confirmed inquiries, not a guaranteed percentage uplift. Investigate indexing if absent, relevance if exposure is weak, snippets only with sufficient comparable exposure, contact friction if visits fail to progress, and carrier receipt if handoffs do not become conversations. Expand only from evidence and explicit publication eligibility.
